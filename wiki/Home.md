## 🌟 Key Engineering Capabilities

1. **Outcome-Based Education (OBE) Integration**: Direct mapping of questions to Course Outcomes (COs) and Program Outcomes (POs) with performance indicators.
2. **Pedagogical Cognitive Blueprinting**: Algorithmic distribution of marks across cognitive levels:
   - **L1** Remember &bull; **L2** Understand &bull; **L3** Apply
   - **L4** Analyze &bull; **L5** Evaluate &bull; **L6** Create
3. **Multi-Provider Resilient LLM Tier**: Zero-downtime dynamic fallback across free-tier inference APIs (Groq Cloud, OpenRouter, Cerebras Cloud, and Mistral AI).
4. **Algorithmic Technical Schematics**: In-engine procedural SVG synthesis for circuit diagrams, architecture diagrams, neural networks, decision trees, and experimental tables.
5. **Memory-Efficient SQLite Data Layer**: Disk-backed relational persistence for faculty profiles, sessions, and examination archives using WAL mode.
6. **Institutional DOCX Export**: One-click generation of university-grade Microsoft Word papers complete with official headers, formatting, and OBE compliance matrices.
